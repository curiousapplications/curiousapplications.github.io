GAIN Fitness wanted a polished, AI-driven app that builds a personalized workout every day around each user's goals, experience, injuries, and time. We built it from scratch, from guided onboarding to progress tracking. Users set up the equipment they have, so workouts fit anywhere from the living room to the gym, and can skip an exercise when equipment is busy and come back to it later. The result was an experience that adapts to each user and their setup.
