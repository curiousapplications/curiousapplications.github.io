Methodist Le Bonheur Healthcare wanted to give hospital administrators drill-down dashboards on care quality and efficiency, from length of stay to readmissions. We engineered the app on a small set of reusable frameworks, so it could grow to many complex, data-heavy screens and deliver parts of it to other devices, laying the foundation for future projects.
