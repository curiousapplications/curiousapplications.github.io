Twine, a savings app later acquired by John Hancock, brought us in to support their team and expand their analytics. Instead of hand-writing hundreds of analytics events, we generated them from Twine's own specification, saving months of work and eliminating a whole class of bugs. That approach powered the app's analytics for four years, until the app was retired.
