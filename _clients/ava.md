Ava's app lets deaf and hard-of-hearing people follow group conversations. Ava brought us in to rescue their pre-launch iOS app, which was behind schedule with an aggressive holiday launch ahead. We redesigned the protocol behind group sessions, simplified the codebase, and hired and mentored a successor engineer. We got them back on track and shipped on time for Thanksgiving. Within two weeks, Ava had 100K users.
